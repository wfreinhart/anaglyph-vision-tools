Anaglyph vision tools

Two single-file web apps for red/green (anaglyph) vision exercises. Put the filter glasses on first. Each target can be inked so that only one eye can see it, which is what makes these training tools rather than ordinary eye charts. Calibrate to your own glasses before use — the defaults will not match them.

Shape Hunt Jigsaw, blocks, shape sorter and touch modes for a small child. Letter trainer Anaglyph letter chart with a calibration wizard.

Not a medical device. Built for one child at their eye doctor's direction and shared in case it is useful. Anything you do with it is between you and your own clinician.
